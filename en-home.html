About us

Experience comfort and relaxation at Hotel Alte Innbrücke!

Location

Hotel Alte Innbrücke is only 10 km from the famous cathedral city of Passau (the cathedral houses the largest church organ in the world), at the confluence of the Danube, Inn and Ilz rivers, surrounded by forests. The Gasthaus Innblick restaurant is just one minute away on foot. The Austrian baroque town of Schärding is only a 10-minute walk away, and the Inn river is reachable in 8 minutes. Passau is 22 minutes by car, and Schärding main station is 2,600 metres from the hotel.

Comfort

The hotel has 37 comfortable, bright rooms. The rooms are modern and equipped with bathroom, hairdryer, TV, fridge and WiFi. Some rooms have their own balcony or kitchen. Parking, WiFi and Self-Check-In Non-Stop service are included in the room prices.

Convenient location

The hotel is located in a quiet and beautiful area with a wonderful view of the Inn river, offering guests a wide range of services and facilities for comfort and relaxation. It is the perfect place for an ideal holiday and a starting point for wonderful hikes and day trips to neighbouring Austria, the Bavarian Forest and the spa triangle of Bad Füssing, Bad Griesbach and Bad Birnbach.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Here you will find answers to frequently asked questions about our hotel policies, check-in procedures and amenities.

I booked but did not receive a confirmation! What should I do?

Please first check the SPAM folder in your inbox. If you have not received the confirmation, please contact us by email.

How can I book a hotel room?

Reservations can be made online. You can find the best price guarantee directly on our booking page.

Will I receive a booking confirmation?

Yes, you will receive a booking confirmation. You will usually find it in your inbox within 10 minutes. For bookings made through a booking portal, you will always receive the confirmation from the portal and also from our hotel.

From what time can I check in on the day of arrival?

Reserved rooms are available from 13:00 on the day of arrival.

When do I need to check out on departure day?

You must check out by 10:30 on departure day. Failure to do so may result in an additional day charge. You do not need to sign out — just make sure you have not left any belongings and close the doors.
